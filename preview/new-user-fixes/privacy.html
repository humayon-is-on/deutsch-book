What we collect

  • Without an account (guest): nothing leaves your device. Your progress is kept only while the app is open.
  • With an account: your email address, your name if you sign in with Google, and your learning progress (level, completed lessons, quiz scores, saved words and app settings).

Why we collect it

Only to sign you in and keep your progress safe, so you can continue on any device. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in a data centre in Sydney, Australia. Data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules ensure that each account can only read its own progress. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email and name with us under Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time: open the app, go to Profile → Account and data → Delete account permanently. Your account and all saved progress are removed from our servers straight away. See how to delete your account for other ways.

Children

The app is a general language-learning app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For any question about your data, contact us through the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
